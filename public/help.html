Hearth

Getting Started

Welcome! Hearth is where your groups hang out: text channels, voice and video calls, screen sharing for games, and direct messages. This guide walks through everything you can do. In the app, tap ? at the bottom left anytime to replay the tour.

1. Your account

Signing up

  1. Choose a username (letters, numbers, dots and underscores) and a display name (what people see).
  2. Enter your email. It's used only to verify you and to reset your password.
  3. Enter your date of birth. Hearth is for people 13 and older, and some channels are 18+ or 21+. You can only set it once, so get it right. It's encrypted and never shown to anyone.
  4. Pick a password of at least 12 characters. Tip: a few random words, like purple tractor fishing trip, are strong and easy to remember.

Verify your email

We'll send you a 6-digit code. Enter it when asked, or later from the yellow bar at the top of the app.

Never get locked out

  • Forgot your password? On the login screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your username or email, and use the code we email you.
  • Backup recovery codes: go to Profile → Account recovery → Create codes. Save the 10 codes somewhere safe (a password manager is ideal). Each one can reset your password once, even if you lose your email.
  • Changing your password signs out your other devices automatically.

2. Finding your way around

  • Far left: the flame is your direct messages. Below it, each cartridge-shaped icon is a server you're in. + creates or joins a server.
  • Channel list: the server's channels. # channels are for text, and speaker icons are voice channels. A dot means unread messages.
  • Middle: the conversation and the message box.
  • Right: who's in the server, split into online and offline. On a phone, tap the people icon at the top right.
  • Bottom left: your profile, the ? help button, and log out.

On a phone, tap ☰ to open the server and channel list.

3. Servers and invites

  • Join a server: open an invite link someone sent you, or tap + → Join with an invite and paste it.
  • Create your own: tap + → Create my own, give it a name, and you're the owner. You get a #general text channel and a General voice channel to start.
  • Invite friends: open the server's gear icon → Invite, or type /invite in chat. Links last 7 days.
  • Nickname: you can use a different name in each server: gear icon → Overview, or /nick NewName.
  • Leave a server: gear icon → Overview → Leave server.

4. Chatting

  • Send: type and press Enter. Shift+Enter starts a new line.
  • Files and pictures: drag them into the window, paste them, or tap + next to the message box (up to 25 MB each).
  • GIFs: tap GIF next to the message box, or type /gif party. Pick one and it's sent. GIFs come from GIPHY; you can turn off automatic loading in your profile.
  • Archived messages: in servers moved from Discord, old messages show the original sender's name with an ARCHIVED tag. They aren't linked to anyone's Hearth account.
  • Hover a message (or tap it on a phone) for actions: 🙂 react, ↩ reply, ✎ edit your own, 🗑 delete, ⚑ report.
  • Edit your last message: press ↑ in an empty message box.
  • Mention someone: type @username. They get a notification.
  • Older messages: scroll up and they load automatically.

5. Slash commands

Type / at the start of a message to see the command menu. Use ↑ ↓ to choose and Tab or Enter to pick.

CommandWhat it does
/helpLists every command you can use here.
/me wavesPosts an action: "* Josh waves".
/roll 2d6Rolls dice (d20, 3d8+2 and so on). The server rolls, so nobody can fake it.
/flipFlips a coin.

Moderators get more, like /timeout, /kick, /ban, /slowmode and /purge. See the Server Admin guide.

6. Voice, video and screen sharing

  1. Join: tap a voice channel (speaker icon). Your browser asks for microphone permission the first time.
  2. Controls appear at the bottom of the channel list: 🎙 mute, 🎧 deafen (mute everyone else too), 📷 camera, 🖥 share your screen, ✕ leave.
  3. See everyone: tap the voice channel again for the full view with cameras and screen shares. A green ring shows who's talking.

Streaming games

  • Screen sharing runs up to 1080p at 60 fps, free for everyone.
  • To include game sound, share from Chrome or Edge on Windows, or use the desktop app and leave Share sound ticked.
  • Pick a single window or game instead of your whole screen to keep notifications private.

Voice and video need a secure (https) connection. If the microphone is blocked, check your browser's site permissions.

7. Direct messages

  • Tap the flame at the top left, then + and enter a username. Or tap someone in a server's member list and choose Message.
  • Start a voice or video call from the 📞 button at the top of a DM.
  • People you've blocked can't DM you.

8. Phone and desktop apps

  • Phone: open Hearth in Safari or Chrome, then use Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or ⋮ → Install app (Android). It opens full-screen like an app.
  • Windows desktop app: install it and enter your Hearth's address. It adds a tray icon, notifications, a screen picker with game audio, and hotkeys that work in games: Ctrl+Shift+M mute and Ctrl+Shift+D deafen.
  • Any computer: in Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar.

9. Make it yours

Open your profile (bottom left):

  • Appearance: choose one of the retro palettes (Phosphor, Pocket Classic, Twilight 16, Neon Arcade, Dungeon Crawl, Vector Space, Pixel Plains, Lava Castle) and Dark, Grey, Light or Auto. It's saved per device.
  • Retro sound effects for joining and leaving voice, muting and mentions. Turn them off if you prefer.
  • Change your display name and profile picture.

10. Safety and privacy

  • Report: hover a message → ⚑, or tap a person → Report. Pick what's wrong. Reports go to the server's moderators. Anything about child safety goes straight to the Hearth safety team. The person never sees who reported them.
  • Block: tap a person → Block. Their messages are hidden from you (you can still peek), and they can't DM you. Manage blocks in Profile → Blocked users.
  • Age-restricted channels show an 18+ or 21+ tag and only appear for people old enough. You'll see a warning the first time you open one.
  • Signed-in devices: Profile → Signed-in devices shows where you're logged in. Sign out anything you don't recognize, then change your password.
  • Your data: Profile → Download my data gives you a copy of everything stored about you. Delete account removes your account and everything you've posted.
  • Read the Privacy Policy and Terms for the full details.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services first (911 in the US). If you're struggling, you can call or text 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) anytime.
