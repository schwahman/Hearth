Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

The short version: we collect only what's needed to run chat, voice and video and to keep people safe. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking. Your password is never stored in readable form, and your email and date of birth are stored encrypted. When you delete something, it's gone from Hearth immediately; for safety and legal reasons an encrypted copy is kept for 30 days and then erased.

Who runs this service

Hearth at this address is operated by [OPERATOR NAME] ("we", "us"). Questions or requests about your data: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account details: your username, display name, optional profile picture, and when you signed up and agreed to these terms.
  • Email address (stored encrypted): used only to verify your account, send password-reset codes, and alert you to security changes such as a new password. We don't send marketing email.
  • Date of birth (stored encrypted): used only to confirm you're at least 13 and to decide whether you can see 18+ or 21+ channels. It's never shown to anyone.
  • Your password, never in readable form. We keep only a one-way scrambled version (an scrypt hash). Nobody, including us, can turn it back into your password. Backup recovery codes are stored the same way.
  • What you post: messages, files, reactions, and the servers, channels, roles and nicknames you create or join.
  • Signed-in devices: browser or app type, IP address and last-active time for each sign-in, so you can review and sign out devices.
  • Safety records: reports you make or that are made about your content, moderation actions taken in servers, and failed login attempts (username tried and IP address).

We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, contacts, or payment details. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking tools and set no third-party cookies. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

Voice, video and screen sharing

Calls connect directly between participants' devices and are always encrypted in transit (WebRTC with DTLS-SRTP). Our server only helps devices find each other. Calls are never recorded or stored. If a relay is needed to connect a call, it passes encrypted data through without recording it.

How your data is protected

  • Data travels encrypted between your device and our server over HTTPS.
  • Email addresses, dates of birth and the retention vault (below) are encrypted at rest with AES-256.
  • Messages and files are stored on our server and are not end-to-end encrypted. People who can see a channel can read it. Our safety team can access stored data only to investigate reports, handle abuse, or meet legal obligations, and every such access is recorded in an internal log.
  • Your sign-in uses a secure cookie that page scripts can't read; we store only a scrambled copy of it.
  • Uploaded files are only shown to signed-in people who can see that channel.

Where your data is stored

On servers we rent from [HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Vultr] in [LOCATION, e.g. Chicago, Illinois, USA]. Email is sent through [EMAIL PROVIDER], which receives only the address and the message we send. These providers don't use your data for their own purposes.

Who can see what

  • Messages in a server channel are visible to members who can view that channel. Private and age-restricted channels are visible only to the people allowed in.
  • Direct messages are visible to the people in that conversation.
  • Server owners and moderators can delete messages, time out, kick or ban members, and see reports made in their server (except child-safety reports, which only Hearth's safety team sees).
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data, except as described under "Law enforcement and legal requests".

How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: until you delete them.
  • Deleted messages, files, channels, servers and accounts: removed from Hearth immediately and can't be restored. An encrypted copy is kept in a secure retention vault for 30 days, accessible only to Hearth's safety team, so that abuse and illegal activity can still be investigated. It is then erased automatically. Earlier versions of edited messages are kept the same way.
  • Reported content: a copy is preserved for 1 year from the report, as US law requires for child-safety reports (18 U.S.C. 2258A).
  • Legal holds: if we receive a valid legal preservation request (for example under 18 U.S.C. 2703(f)) or expect legal action, related data is kept until the matter is resolved.
  • Uploads that are never posted: removed after 24 hours. Sign-ins: expire after 30 days unused. Server moderation logs: 90 days. System logs: 30 days.

Law enforcement and legal requests

  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as US law requires, and preserve the related data.
  • We disclose information to government authorities only when legally required (for example a subpoena, court order or search warrant), or when we believe in good faith that someone faces imminent danger of death or serious physical injury, as US law permits.
  • Where the law allows, we tell affected users about requests for their data.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Profile → Download my data.
  • Correct it: edit your display name, picture, email, nicknames and messages at any time. If your date of birth is wrong, contact us.
  • Delete it: delete messages, or Profile → Delete account. Deletion is immediate on Hearth; the encrypted safety copy described above is erased after 30 days unless a report or legal hold requires longer.
  • Control your sign-ins: Profile → Signed-in devices. Block people: from their profile.

Deleting your account removes what you posted. Messages other people wrote belong to them; ask them or a server moderator to remove those, or report them.

Children

Hearth is for people 13 and older. We ask for date of birth at sign-up and don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete it. Age-restricted (18+ and 21+) channels are hidden from anyone whose date of birth shows they're too young.

If something goes wrong

If a security breach affects your data, we'll notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required by law, explaining what happened and what we're doing about it.

Changes to this policy

We'll post changes here and update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also announce them in the app.
