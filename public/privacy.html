The short version: we collect only what's needed to run chat, voice and video. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking. Your password is never stored in readable form. When you delete something, it's gone permanently.

Who runs this service

Hearth at this address is operated by [OPERATOR NAME] ("we", "us"). Questions or requests about your data: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account details: your username, display name, optional profile picture, and the date you signed up and agreed to these terms.
  • Your password, but never in readable form. We store only a one-way scrambled version (an scrypt hash). Nobody, including us, can turn it back into your password.
  • What you post: messages, files you upload, reactions, and the servers, channels, roles and nicknames you create or join.
  • Signed-in devices: for each device you're signed in on, the browser/app type, IP address and last-active time, so you can review and sign out devices in your profile.
  • Security logs: failed login attempts (username tried and IP address) to detect password-guessing attacks.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or payment details. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and set no third-party cookies. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.

Voice, video and screen sharing

Calls connect directly between participants' devices and are always encrypted in transit (WebRTC with DTLS-SRTP). Our server only helps devices find each other; calls are never recorded or stored. If a relay is needed to connect a call, it passes encrypted data through without recording it.

How your data is protected

  • Data travels encrypted between your device and our server over HTTPS.
  • Messages and files are stored on our server; they are not end-to-end encrypted. People who can see a channel can read it, and the server's administrators could technically access stored data. We only do so to keep the service running, investigate abuse reports, or when legally required.
  • Your sign-in uses a secure cookie that page scripts can't read; we store only a scrambled copy of it.
  • Uploaded files are only shown to signed-in people who can see that channel.
  • Repeated wrong passwords temporarily lock an account to stop guessing.

Where your data is stored

On servers we rent from [HOSTING PROVIDER, e.g. Vultr] in [LOCATION, e.g. Chicago, Illinois, USA]. The hosting provider supplies the hardware and doesn't use your data for its own purposes.

Who can see what

  • Messages in a server channel are visible to members who can view that channel.
  • Direct messages are visible to the people in that conversation.
  • Server owners and moderators can delete messages and remove members in their servers.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone, except where the law requires us to (for example, a valid court order).

How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: until you delete them.
  • Deleted messages, files, channels, servers and accounts: removed immediately and permanently. Deleted data is overwritten in our database, and we keep no backup copies.
  • Uploads that are never posted: removed automatically after 24 hours.
  • Sign-ins: expire after 30 days of no use (one year at most).
  • Server logs: kept for up to 30 days.

If we ever start keeping backups, we'll update this policy first and say how long backups last before they expire.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data: Profile → Download my data gives you everything we store about you as a file.
  • Correct it: edit your display name, picture, nicknames and messages at any time.
  • Delete it: delete individual messages, or Profile → Delete account to permanently erase your account and everything you've posted. This cannot be undone.
  • Control your sign-ins: Profile → Signed-in devices.

Deleting your account removes what you posted. Messages other people wrote, even if they mention you, belong to them; ask them or a server moderator to remove those.

Children

Hearth is for people 13 and older. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete it.

If something goes wrong

If we discover a security breach that affects your data, we will notify affected users as quickly as possible and explain what happened and what we're doing about it.

Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes here and update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also announce them in the app.
